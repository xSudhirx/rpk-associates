About RPK Associates

A Firm Built on Trust and Expertise

RPK Associates is a Bangalore-based Audit and Tax consulting firm specializing in taxation, audit, GST compliance, and business advisory. Founded by R. Praveen Kumar, the firm has built its reputation on delivering accurate, timely, and personalized financial services to clients across industries.

We work with startups, established businesses, salaried individuals, and NRIs to ensure they remain fully compliant while optimizing their financial position. Our approach is hands-on – we don't assign junior staff to handle complex matters; every engagement receives senior-level attention.

Located in the bustling Mahadevapura business district of Bangalore, we serve clients from across the city and are well-versed in the specific compliance needs of IT sector employees, technology startups, and e-commerce businesses concentrated in this corridor.

"Our mission is to make professional financial guidance accessible to every individual and business – not just large corporations. When you work with RPK Associates, you work with people who genuinely care about your financial wellbeing."

— R. Praveen Kumar, Proprietor
